# My Favorite Anime Movies

## Top 3
1. **Weathering with You**
   > Hodaka, a teenage runaway, moves to Tokyo and meets Hina, a girl who can clear the rain and bring out the sun. As they use her power, they learn it comes with a heavy cost.

2. **Your Name**
   > Mitsuha, a girl from a small countryside town, and Taki, a boy in Tokyo, start swapping bodies at random. As they get to know each other's lives, they discover a bigger mystery connecting them.

3. **A Silent Voice**
   > Shoya, a former bully, is haunted by how he treated Shoko, a deaf classmate, in elementary school. Years later, he seeks her out to apologize and tries to make things right.

## Reasons why I like them
- Has depth and good backstory
- Well-crafted characters with good character progression
- Emotional plot twists

>
